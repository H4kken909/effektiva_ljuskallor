# Measurement session (template)

One sheet per measurement session, filled in by hand at the rig. At least two people in the lab for the whole session.

Before the session: date and people, source and diode ID (or lamp), current and voltage, external power meter or lux meter,
camera with exposure and gain, filters in the light path, and ROI, channel and frames (`show`).

Laser: never straight into the slit without attenuation. Use diffuse scattering or a grey filter and the shortest exposure.

After the session: copy the measurement folders to a USB stick and the group's folder with `dev/tools/copy-data.ps1` or `.sh`.
The numbers are relative counts from the camera.
